Research Interests

Computational Catalysis

Reaction mechanisms, transition states, free-energy landscapes and catalyst design.

Bonding & Reactivity

EDA, charge-transfer analysis, conceptual DFT and electronic-structure interpretation.

Photophysics

Excited states, absorption/emission, excitonic interactions and chiroptical properties.

Data-Driven Chemistry

Descriptor-based screening, machine learning and predictive catalyst design.
